Who we are

A rooftop, a braai, and a soccer pitch

Cloud 7 Lifestyle is a two-storey lounge on Steelway — a ground-floor bar for the after-work crowd and a covered rooftop deck upstairs that looks straight over the road onto the stadium. Come for the match, stay for the shisanyama.

Downstairs is built for a slow drink under blue and red light. Upstairs is built for a full house on match day, DJs on weekends, and birthdays with balloon arches out back. Either way, the fryer and the braai stay lit until close.
